  • Lane position: “Crowd inside” must warn about trailer clearance; “Crowd outside” must warn about the outer boundary. Neither banking nor a high-side label authorizes crossing lane lines.
  • Offtracking: The animation is a schematic low-speed inward path. It must not imply every trailer always tracks inward at road speed.
  • Speed: Moving the comparison from 1× to 2× changes relative lateral demand from 1× to 4× at unchanged radius. There must be no green “safe speed,” mph recommendation or rollover threshold.
  • Loaded/empty: Both descriptions retain risk. A high load raises concerns; “empty” must not mean safe to drive faster.
  • Quiz: Choose the “always high side” answer. Next must remain disabled and an explanation must invite retry.
  • Motion: Play/Pause must work; leaving the page must stop motion. With the system reduced-motion setting enabled, the button should step the illustration instead of continuously animating.

3 · Functional walkthrough

  1. Choose each product. Choose the wrong contact direction: installation decisions must remain hidden.
  2. Choose the correct direction, then a wrong decision: Next stays disabled. A correct retry unlocks only the next decision.
  3. Change models halfway through. Orientation and decision progress must reset; the diagrams and manual link must change together.
  4. Inspect the preparation wheel: no installed chain should appear before the draping topic. At the cable tension topic, identify both loops and all seven positions.
  5. Complete both procedures. The final recheck text must remain product-specific, and completion must not claim certification.
  6. Try the Oregon placement example. Both sides of both powered drive axles plus both sides of the same semitrailer axle pass this selected arrangement. A steer selection or split trailer-axle pair must not pass it.
  7. Sign out, then enter again as Synthetic Driver. Neither student quiz progress nor tire selections from the previous visit should remain.

4 · Regulation and accessibility acceptance checks

Every state must show a review status, source evidence and unresolved fields. A limited permission excerpt is not a fully reviewed carry/placement rule. Check current agency road controls separately, including vehicle category and timestamp.

  • Filter Fully reviewed: an empty result is honest when no full review has passed.
  • Check unresolved Georgia, Michigan and Oklahoma entries for explicit holds; do not treat a research lead as a verified rule.
  • Use only Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter and arrow keys. Tire selection must retain focus on the same control and announce its pressed state.
  • Check the page at narrow widths and 200% browser zoom. Labels, correct/incorrect explanations, diagrams and Next controls must remain usable.
  • Confirm that explanatory text accompanies color and that diagrams have accessible names. Perform actual VoiceOver/TalkBack testing before accessibility acceptance.
