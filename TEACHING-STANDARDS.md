# Teaching standards — Maricel CDL Success Hub

## Authorship and audience

Original educational content and visual learning materials by Coach Maricel — Maricel CDL Success Hub. Use original explanations, activities, layouts and custom learning diagrams with the existing Maricel artwork. Credit the official guidance used to check the principles; do not describe government guidance or shared vehicle-control principles as exclusive Hub inventions. No school materials or official exam diagrams may be reproduced without permission. Member pages describe the material directly, without revision announcements.

Teach zero-experience beginners. Define terms before relying on them, state the driver viewpoint, and distinguish the pictured reference from a real view or measured movement. Current backing material concerns a left-hand-drive tractor and one semitrailer.

## Evidence hierarchy and geographic scope

1. FMCSA ELDT overview and official curriculum summary provide national training context. They do not approve the Hub, establish its illustrations or award learners ELDT/behind-the-wheel credit.
2. Shared backing principles are cross-checked against official guidance from more than one jurisdiction, currently California DMV and Washington DOL. Cite those sources as general operational references. Do not re-label a state manual as federal law or imply every detail applies nationwide.
3. Exact licensing/test requirements belong to the learner's current state manual and testing location. Verify exercises, test version, cone/boundary dimensions, scoring, permitted looks/pull-ups, inspection and finish instructions locally. California's modernized test information is labeled an example, not a nationwide standard.
4. Equipment and site instructions govern the actual setup, transmission, parking/exit procedure, assistance and loading-facility process. OSHA spotter guidance is conditional on using a spotter; dockboard rules do not create a generic dock-contact procedure.
5. Link claims to sources in `dist/disclaimer.html#learning-source-credits`; use the source/scope panels throughout Lessons, Backing Guides, Practice Yard and poster notes. Keep source names and the companion URL within downloadable resources.

## Content review checks

- Identify intentional turn from alignment versus confirmed unwanted straight-back drift before giving an initial top-of-wheel direction. Following, easing and real response are separate decisions. An already-angled combination needs assessment.
- Separate tractor/trailer alignment, trailer/lane alignment and lane-center position. Wheel center does not prove these or guarantee the next path.
- Distinguish rear corners, rear wheel group, tractor-front sweep, both side clearances, hidden rear area and actual stopping location. Do not imply all references are visible at once or that identical mirror gaps prove alignment.
- Explain stopping and securing before exit. Parking brake(s), appropriate transmission setting and the complete demonstrated procedure matter; the service/foot-brake pedal alone is not parking. A pull-up needs a newly checked path and plan.
- Draw driver-left/right consistently. Every illustrative pose/path must be labeled for what it actually represents. No invented safe angles, steering counts, delays, clearance margins or physical response calculations.
- Questions need one supported answer, plausible alternatives and a consistent explanation/retry. Screen review is not practical readiness or a certification result.
- Keep information current, conditional and appropriately scoped. No mastery/safety guarantees, whole-nation bridge-inventory claims or universal parking/timing rules.
- Before publishing teaching changes: inspect the source and latest deployment, check changed claims against primary sources, review diagram directions and the resulting learner flow, run meaningful existing checks and applicable regressions, preserve member data, synchronize development and publish through the existing Site.

## Hub presentation and coaching voice

Use the original Hub learning frame **Notice → Understand → Decide → Check** as an organizing aid, not a claim of a new physical law, exclusive backing method or certified result. Coach Maricel’s explanations help learners describe a reference, identify the situation, discuss a response with qualified guidance and observe again. Stopping remains an available decision whenever the path or next move is unclear.

Foundation lesson prose, headings, coaching prompts and selected question prompts were reframed in the Hub’s own learner-first voice. Existing exercise keys, 12 lesson answer choices/answer indices, direction distinctions, artwork, glossary safeguards, review gating and local-test limits were preserved. The learning frame also appears in the Backing Guides and poster companion introduction; Practice Yard describes its role as scenario study before supervised equipment practice. Source notes remain separate and credited. This copy work does not establish legal clearance or exclusive rights over facts and shared vehicle-control principles.

## Air-brake guide

Keep applied/static tests separate and always identify configuration, engine/control state, stabilization and timing. Preserve state-format warning distinctions (modernized CA/WA example55 vs NY general60), actual manufacturer criteria and roll-prevention/recharge prerequisites. Do not turn study answers into actual inspection completion, defect clearance or practical certification. No pressure-depletion exercise while driving, equipment bypass or DIY brake repairs.
