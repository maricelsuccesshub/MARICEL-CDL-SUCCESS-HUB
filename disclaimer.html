Educational purpose

Created by Coach Maricel, this Hub offers explanations, study activities and refreshers for new and experienced drivers. Follow qualified instruction, your employer’s procedures, applicable laws and your vehicle’s limitations.

Your discretion and responsibility

Use of this Hub is voluntary. Every student, trainee and driver is responsible for deciding whether information applies to their experience, vehicle, load, route and current conditions, and whether it is safe and lawful to act. Do not attempt a maneuver or continue a trip merely because an example, exercise, suggestion, camera image or link appears here. If information is unclear, conflicts with official instructions, or cannot be independently verified, do not rely on it; stop in a suitable location and seek qualified guidance.

The Hub does not inspect your vehicle, assess your readiness, supervise a maneuver, monitor your surroundings or make driving decisions for you. Its content is general and is not individualized instruction, dispatch direction, legal advice, emergency assistance or an assurance that a situation is safe. Completing an activity does not establish competence or authorization to operate a commercial vehicle.

Low bridges and clearance checks

Clearance resources are supplemental pre-trip references, not a complete bridge inventory, route survey, truck navigation system or permission to pass. Confirm actual vehicle, trailer, load and equipment height during pre-trip using appropriate procedures and qualified help. Check current official records for the correct route, lane and direction; include local roads, ramps, detours and final approaches. Recheck after equipment or configuration changes and obey posted restrictions. A pre-trip inspection or a missing database listing does not establish safe clearance. If uncertain, stop safely before the obstruction and contact the responsible road authority and your carrier. Maricel CDL Success Hub does not measure structures or guarantee clearance.

Low-bridge & clearance source credits

Maricel CDL Success Hub links to the following official resources for pre-trip planning. The agencies provide and maintain their own information; the Hub does not measure bridges or certify clearance. These are external links, not copied or embedded bridge maps. Names identify the sources and do not imply affiliation, sponsorship or endorsement.

Source links reviewed: October 28, 2026. This is the Hub’s review date, not the date each bridge was measured or its data last updated. Always check the source’s current notices.

  • California — Caltrans
    Open Caltrans low-clearance list · Temporary restrictions & permits

    Lists state-route clearances at or below 14 feet. Local roads beneath state routes are excluded; measurement position can differ from roadside postings.

  • Texas — TxDOT / TxDMV
    Open Texas clearance map · Open district low-clearance lists

    The accompanying TxDMV district lists cover structures at or below 14 feet. Source information may lag physical changes; check current restrictions.

  • Washington — Washington State DOT
    Open WSDOT clearance planner

    Local roads and streets beneath state routes are excluded. Published data does not guarantee physical clearance or identify every lane condition.

  • Indiana — Indiana DOT
    Open Indiana bridge-clearance map · INDOT map instructions & restrictions

    Covers interstates, U.S. highways and state routes. City streets and county roads require local-authority verification.

  • Illinois — Illinois DOT
    Open Illinois restrictions map

    State-system map data is a reference and may not cover local-road restrictions. A bridge’s load limit is not its overhead clearance.

  • Oregon — Oregon DOT
    Open Oregon Route Map 3 (PDF) · ODOT route-map guidance

    State-highway height-routing reference. Ramps, access roads and local structures need separate verification.

  • Pennsylvania — PennDOT / 511PA
    Open 511PA low-bridge map

    Low Bridge coverage is over state routes and excludes PA Turnpike roadways. Overheight or overweight permit routing uses APRAS, not the general 511 map.

  • Massachusetts — Massachusetts DOT
    Open MassDOT Truck Height Check

    Checks the routes and tunnels listed by the tool. It is not an inventory of every low bridge in Massachusetts.

  • New York — New York State DOT
    Read NYSDOT low-bridge parkway warning · NYC commercial-traffic route guidance

    This is a parkway hazard advisory, not a statewide list of bridge heights. NYC commercial-route guidance is also available; verify other roads with their responsible authority.

  • Federal Highway Administration (FHWA)
    State oversize/overweight permit and routing contacts

    Directory for finding the responsible state permit office. It is not a bridge-clearance database or route approval. Confirm contact details and state links with the current agency.

California district PDF maps: Caltrans also maintains the linked truck-route maps for Districts 2, 3, 4, 7 and 11. These are direct external PDF links from its low-clearance table. Check each document’s own revision date; linking today does not mean the map was updated today.

Coverage is incomplete: The selector includes all 50 states and DC. Dedicated resources or advisories are currently linked for the nine states above. Other selections explicitly require further clearance verification and offer general road-condition links from the state agency/511 providers already credited above. A general road map is not a clearance inventory. Local streets, ramps, detours, lane differences and temporary changes may be missing.
