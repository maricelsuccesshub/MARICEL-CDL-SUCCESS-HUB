## Review holds

- **Colorado:** Source discrepancy: an older CDOT overview still lists only I-70 Morrison–Dotsero. Do not use that shorter route description. Confirm borderline weight classifications with CSP; this overview is not a complete installation rule.
- **Georgia:** An older DPS handout describes four drive-tire chains, while newer statutory reproductions use outermost-drive-tire wording. Current authoritative wording and applicability need reconciliation before a placement rule is shown.
- **Michigan:** The patrol FAQ permits chains for safety but also says chains must not contact the road surface. Obtain clarification before presenting a practical truck-use rule.
- **Oklahoma:** The official statute text includes vehicle-capacity and seasonal qualifications whose scope needs review. No general truck permission is asserted.
- **Utah:** UDOT business and public summaries use slightly different weight/class wording; use the current rule/order for borderline cases.
- **Washington:** Other WSDOT summary pages use differing season wording. This entry uses the regulation; check its current route list and exceptions.

## Manufacturer and driving references

- [Peerless/SCC Quick Grip CAM Truck Singles](https://peerlesschain.com/download/2016/08/12/9ue09h70ae_Quick_Grip_CAM_Truck_Singles.pdf): orientation, outside CAM placement, connectors, tension, loose links and initial retightening.
- [Peerless/SCC Super Z Single](https://peerlesschain.com/download/2016/08/09/ufkmarqwh_Super_Z_single.pdf): smooth contact faces, cable fasteners and the specified tensioners. The manual has a different adjustment procedure from CAM chains.
- Both manufacturer documents contain legacy braking advice. The app does not reproduce that advice; follow current vehicle braking/ABS instructions and obtain qualified equipment instruction.
- [FHWA roadway geometry/offtracking](https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/policy/otps/truck/wusr/chap06.cfm): distinguish low-speed inward and high-speed outward effects.
- [California DMV truck guidance](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/driver-education-and-safety/special-interest-driver-guides/truck-drivers/): reduce speed before curves.
- [FMCSA rollover prevention](https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/rolloverprevention): load, vehicle, road and driver factors.

No universal chain speed, distance-to-recheck, manufacturer sequence or high-side lane-position rule is asserted. The curve drawing is illustrative, not a vehicle dynamics or safe-speed model.
